Forty Feet Down From Forever

4 July 1925, Beach Street, Boston

Listen: Petunia's Peculiarium on Spotify.

The story

In the early hours of 4 July 1925, the Pickwick Club, a second-floor nightclub on Beach Street in Boston, was crowded with dancers. The building had been damaged by fire, the building next door had been torn down, and a deep excavation for its replacement ran alongside. At about half past three in the morning, the building collapsed into that excavation. Forty-four people died.

Record and legend

What the record shows

  • Forty-four people died. (1, 3)
  • Twelve men were indicted, among them the building inspector, contractors, and the club's operators. None was convicted. (3)
  • Civil suits sought about $6.1 million and were settled, all together, for $40,000. (1)

What the legend adds

  • That the Charleston brought the building down. Dancing was blamed at the time, and there was talk of banning the dance, but the building's condition and the excavation next door were the deeper causes. (2)
