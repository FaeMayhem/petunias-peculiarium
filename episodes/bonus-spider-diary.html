The Spider Diary

22 August 1818, Washington

Listen: Listen to this episode on Spotify

The story

On 22 August 1818, John Quincy Adams, then Secretary of State under President James Monroe, wrote in his diary that he had spent the night awake with his skin crawling. In the morning he found the cause: a nest of newly hatched spiders in his linen. He changed his clothes, took a warm bath, and then rode to his office and called on the Attorney General, William Wirt, about the Navigation Act.

Adams kept his diary for more than sixty years. The Massachusetts Historical Society holds it, and the full text is free to read online.
