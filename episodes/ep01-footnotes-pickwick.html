What's in it

Marie steps out from behind Petunia to cover what Episode 1 left out: the police officer tracking a jewel thief inside the Pickwick Club when it collapsed, the warnings that were laughed off, the rescue that went on through the night, the trial that convicted no one, and a possible link to H. P. Lovecraft. The main episode is Forty Feet Down From Forever.
