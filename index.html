A podcast of macabre history

Petunia's Peculiarium

Bodies, disasters, legends, and the people who were there.

Each episode, Petunia Pickman lays a true story from the past on her table and examines it. Season 1 begins at home, in Boston, and ventures out across New England. This site holds what wouldn't fit in your ears: the images, the documents, and every source, so you can check the record yourself.
